Week 6 · Set A · Design specification

ASCII Dropdown

Tone: Monochrome cobalt blue on cream — Young Serif section headers, Space Mono uppercase body, thick-ruled grid sections like a printed cafe menu card.

Anatomy

Thick-bordered card with horizontal rules dividing sections. Each section opens with a large serif heading. Three-column item grid: icon (16 px) · label (fluid, uppercase mono) · trailing area (auto). Footer with version and section count.
