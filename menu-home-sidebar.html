Week 6 · Style 2 · Design specification

Home Sidebar Expanding

Tone: iOS Home sidebar — SF Pro, room-based accordion sections with colored icons, device rows with status dots, spring entrance stagger, environment stats and brightness slider at footer.

Anatomy

A fixed-width sidebar panel with a header, five expandable room sections, and a footer with environment stats and a brightness slider. Each section accordion-reveals device rows with status indicators.
