Week 6 · Set B · Design specification

Sidebar Expanding

Tone: Accordion sidebar in cobalt blue on cream — Young Serif section toggles, Space Mono uppercase items, thick-ruled sections with animated expand/collapse.

Anatomy

Thick-bordered sidebar panel with accordion sections. Each section has a serif toggle header with item count and caret indicator. Items reveal on click with a smooth grid-row animation. Footer with version and section count.
